**GIO-1 0.3.1-alpha: the drums' echo much stronger.**

A small update to 0.3-alpha. GIO-1 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a generative electronic disco machine. Try it in the
browser first: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/emu/

> [!CAUTION]
> **Disclaimer: installing this firmware could brick your FM-1.** GIO-1 is unofficial, independent firmware, not
> made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. It is an alpha. You install it entirely at your own risk; the author
> accepts no responsibility for any damage to your device, including a device that no longer starts.

> [!WARNING]
> **Tested on two FM-1s.** 0.3.1-alpha has been installed on two separate FM-1s and works on both, with m-bootloader
> installed on both too. With 0.3-alpha, other firmware was installed over GIO-1 on each and GIO-1 put back again. It
> has not yet been tried more widely: expect problems, and please report what happens in the Issues.

> [!NOTE]
> **The safety nets** (from fm1-base, unchanged):
> - **A crash** shows a red CRASH screen, and the FM-1 restarts.
> - **Two failed starts in a row:** SAFE MODE, with no sound and USB still on, so you can install again or put
>   M-VAVE's firmware back.
> - **If safe mode fails too:** the chip's own update mode, from which the FM-1 rescue tool
>   (`base/bsp/tools/fm1_rescue.sh`, macOS) puts M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
> - **Update mode by hand:** hold REC while switching the FM-1 on, until the screen says UPDATE MODE. With m-bootloader installed, the screen
>   stays black instead: that is its update mode (the same chip's mode). Switch off and on again to leave it.
> - **Back to stock:** the installer's "Back to the official firmware", with M-VAVE's own `FM-1.fwsc`.

**Changes since 0.3-alpha:**
- **The drums' Echo** (Drums FX, KNOB 1) comes up much more above 60. Only the snare and tom go into the echo, and
  even at 100 it used to be faint, about 21 dB under the drums; now it rises fast past 60 and at 100 is nearly as
  loud as the drums themselves (4 dB under them). A limiter before the tape keeps a loud snare from distorting it,
  even with Feedback at 100.
- **The songs sound exactly as before:** their own settings are all 60 or under, where nothing has changed.

Your saved songs are kept when you install it over 0.3-alpha (or earlier). A song you saved with the drums' Echo
above 60 will have more drum echo than before; turn it down to taste.

**Package:** `gio-0.3.1-alpha.fwsc` (its identity `FM-1_58` and the version), built and tested on GitHub by the shared FM-1 builder, with signed build provenance (see BUILDING.md to verify it).

GIO-1 is a fork of AMB-1 by Charles Vestal, built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp (Hügelton Instruments). MPL-2.0.
