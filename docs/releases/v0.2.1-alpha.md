**GIO-1 0.2.1-alpha: the pad's Choir as loud as Strings and Saws.**

A small update to 0.2-alpha. GIO-1 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a generative electronic disco machine. Try it in the
browser first: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/emu/

> [!CAUTION]
> **Disclaimer: installing this firmware could brick your FM-1.** GIO-1 is unofficial, independent firmware, not
> made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. It is an alpha. You install it entirely at your own risk; the author
> accepts no responsibility for any damage to your device, including a device that no longer starts.

> [!WARNING]
> **Tested on one FM-1 only.** 0.2-alpha works on the one FM-1 it has been tested on; this update changes only the
> pad's Choir level and two presets' settings. It has not yet been tried on other FM-1s. Expect problems; please
> report what happens in the Issues.

> [!NOTE]
> **The safety nets** (from fm1-base, unchanged):
> - **A crash** shows a red CRASH screen, and the FM-1 restarts.
> - **Two failed starts in a row:** SAFE MODE, with no sound and USB still on, so you can install again or put
>   M-VAVE's firmware back.
> - **If safe mode fails too:** the chip's own update mode, from which the FM-1 rescue tool
>   (`base/bsp/tools/fm1_rescue.sh`, macOS) puts M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
> - **Update mode by hand:** hold REC while switching the FM-1 on, until the screen says UPDATE MODE.
> - **Back to stock:** the installer's "Back to the official firmware", with M-VAVE's own `FM-1.fwsc`.

**Changes since 0.2-alpha:**
- **The pad's Choir** was about 7 dB quieter than Strings and Saws at the same Level; now all three are the same.
- **Mutant and Heaven**, the two songs on the Choir, have their Pad level 15% lower (50 to 43, 60 to 51), so their
  pads come up a little without taking over.

Your saved songs are kept when you install it over 0.2-alpha (or 0.1-alpha). A song you've already saved keeps the
Pad level you left it at; SAVE held 1.5 s puts a song back to its recipe.

**Package:** `gio-0.2.1-alpha.fwsc` (its identity `FM-1_58` and the version), built and tested on GitHub by the shared FM-1 builder, with signed build provenance (see BUILDING.md to verify it).

GIO-1 is a fork of AMB-1 by Charles Vestal, built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp (Hügelton Instruments). MPL-2.0.
