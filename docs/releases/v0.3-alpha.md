**GIO-1 0.3-alpha: edit the bassline and the drums, tempo 80 to 150, stronger echo and reverb.**

GIO-1 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a generative electronic disco machine. Try it in the browser first:
https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/emu/

> [!CAUTION]
> **Disclaimer: installing this firmware could brick your FM-1.** GIO-1 is unofficial, independent firmware, not
> made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. It is an alpha. You install it entirely at your own risk; the author
> accepts no responsibility for any damage to your device, including a device that no longer starts.

> [!WARNING]
> **Tested on one FM-1 only.** A test build of this release was installed on one FM-1, where it works; it has not
> yet been tried on others. Expect problems; please report what happens in the Issues.

> [!NOTE]
> **The safety nets** (from fm1-base, unchanged):
> - **A crash** shows a red CRASH screen, and the FM-1 restarts.
> - **Two failed starts in a row:** SAFE MODE, with no sound and USB still on, so you can install again or put
>   M-VAVE's firmware back.
> - **If safe mode fails too:** the chip's own update mode, from which the FM-1 rescue tool
>   (`base/bsp/tools/fm1_rescue.sh`, macOS) puts M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
> - **Update mode by hand:** hold REC while switching the FM-1 on, until the screen says UPDATE MODE.
> - **Back to stock:** the installer's "Back to the official firmware", with M-VAVE's own `FM-1.fwsc`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Your saved songs are kept** when you install 0.3-alpha over 0.2.1-alpha (or earlier): their settings and chords
> as you left them, the new bass patterns and drum grids each song's own. Going back to an earlier release
> afterwards resets them to the defaults (it can't read the newer save).

**New in 0.3-alpha:**
- **EDIT now has three pages**, one a press: Bassline, Chords, Drum grid.
- **The Bassline page:** the bass pattern playing as a piano roll, in the notes it plays now (chord tones, so it moves
  with the chords). A white key picks a step; KNOB 2 its note (Rest, Root, 3rd, 5th, 7th, Octave, 5th up), KNOB 3
  its accent, KNOB 4 its length (Auto, as the songs play, or tied over up to 16 sixteenths). Evolve's changes show
  dimmed. ALGORITHM picks the pattern edited; each song keeps its own copy of all six.
- **The Drum grid:** kick, snare, hat, open hat and tom over sixteen steps. A white key turns a step on or off for
  the drum KNOB 1 picks; KNOB 3 its level (Off, Soft, Normal, Accent). A song's drums play exactly as before until
  you edit them; the sections still bring the drums in and out, and fills and builds still come.
- **Tempo 80 to 150** (was 110 to 130): HOME + SELECT, or the Song page. The songs keep their own tempos.
- **Echo and Reverb on the FX pages** grow much stronger in the top of their range (above 50 for the reverbs, 60
  for the echoes): the bass's reverb up to 30 dB more at 100. The songs' own settings sound exactly as before.

SAVE held 1.5 s puts a song back to its recipe, its patterns and drums included.

The manual: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/manual/

**Package:** `gio-0.3-alpha.fwsc` (its identity `FM-1_58` and the version), built and tested on GitHub by the shared FM-1 builder, with signed build provenance (see BUILDING.md to verify it).
