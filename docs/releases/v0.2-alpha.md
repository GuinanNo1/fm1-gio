**GIO-1 0.2-alpha: a start-up intro, an FM bass, FX pages for each instrument, a Mixer, and the screen mirror.**

GIO-1 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a generative electronic disco machine: an analog-style (or FM) bass sequence,
strings, a choir or detuned saws through a phaser, a synth lead you can play and answer, four on the floor, nine
presets and sixteen drop-in effects. Try it in the browser first: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/emu/

> [!CAUTION]
> **Disclaimer: installing this firmware could brick your FM-1.** GIO-1 is unofficial, independent firmware, not
> made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. It is an alpha. You install it entirely at your own risk; the author
> accepts no responsibility for any damage to your device, including a device that no longer starts.

> [!WARNING]
> **Tested on one FM-1 only.** A test build of this release was installed on one FM-1, where it works, the screen
> mirror included; it has not yet been tried on others. Before that it was
> tested in fm1-base's simulator, in the browser emulator and against the update installer's code on a simulated
> FM-1 flash. Expect problems; please report what happens in the Issues.

> [!NOTE]
> **The safety nets** (from fm1-base, unchanged since 0.1-alpha):
> - **A crash** shows a red CRASH screen, and the FM-1 restarts.
> - **Two failed starts in a row:** SAFE MODE, with no sound and USB still on, so you can install again or put
>   M-VAVE's firmware back.
> - **If safe mode fails too:** the chip's own update mode, from which the FM-1 rescue tool
>   (`base/bsp/tools/fm1_rescue.sh`, macOS) puts M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
> - **Update mode by hand:** hold REC while switching the FM-1 on, until the screen says UPDATE MODE. (Earlier
>   documents said OCT− and OCT+ for 5 seconds; GIO-1 has never done that. OCT− and OCT+ held at switch-on is the
>   panel's calibration.)
> - **Back to stock:** the installer's "Back to the official firmware", with M-VAVE's own `FM-1.fwsc`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Your saved songs are kept** when you install 0.2-alpha over 0.1-alpha: each song's new settings start from its
> recipe. Going back to 0.1-alpha afterwards resets them to the defaults (0.1-alpha can't read the newer save).

**New in 0.2-alpha:**
- **A start-up intro:** the GIO-1 logo wipes in as a chord rises and lands with a star; then Feel starts. Touch any
  control to skip it.
- **Bass FM** (ENV twice): the bass's voice, Analog or **FM**, with the FM's Ratio, depth and decay. With Analog, the
  page shows the filter's Cutoff, Resonance and Decay.
- **FX pages:** FX goes through Bass FX, Pad FX, Lead FX, Drums FX and Space, each with that instrument's own echo and
  reverb amounts and the echo's Time and Feedback, and the sixteen effects on the white keys on every one.
- **The Mixer** (HOME twice): the four instruments' levels side by side.
- **The screen mirror:** the FM-1's screen and panel live on a computer, on the website's Mirror page (Chrome or
  Edge, USB cable). GLO three times: the Mirror page, to switch it on or off and for the steps.
- **Sound:** the drums a little louder; the Reverb and Wash effects quieter; the Hats knob sets the hats' level too;
  Tape stop winds the whole mix down; Swell builds while held. Stutter and Drop have swapped places.
- **Names:** Emphasis is now Resonance; the ARP button's second page is Lead 2.

The manual: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/manual/
