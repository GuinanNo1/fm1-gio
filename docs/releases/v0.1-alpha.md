**GIO-1 0.1-alpha: the first build for a real M-VAVE FM-1.**

GIO-1 turns the FM-1 into a generative electronic disco machine: a Moog-style bass sequence, strings, a choir or
detuned saws through a phaser, a synth lead you can play and answer, four on the floor, nine presets, sixteen
drop-in effects and a Chords page to rewrite each preset's chord changes. Try it in the browser first:
https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/emu/

> [!CAUTION]
> **Disclaimer: installing this firmware could brick your FM-1.** GIO-1 is unofficial, independent firmware, not
> made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE. It is an alpha. You install it entirely at your own risk; the author
> accepts no responsibility for any damage to your device, including a device that no longer starts.

> [!WARNING]
> **No hardware tests yet.** This build has been tested only in fm1-base's simulator, in the browser emulator and
> against the update installer's code on a simulated FM-1 flash. It has **not yet been run on a real FM-1**.
> Expect problems; please report what happens in the Issues.

> [!NOTE]
> **The safety nets** (from fm1-base, after Melodee's and X0X's):
> - **A crash** shows a red CRASH screen, and the FM-1 restarts.
> - **Two failed starts in a row:** SAFE MODE, with no sound and USB still on, so you can install again or put
>   M-VAVE's firmware back.
> - **If safe mode fails too:** the chip's own update mode, from which the FM-1 rescue tool
>   (`base/bsp/tools/fm1_rescue.sh`, macOS) puts M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
> - **Update mode by hand, at any time:** hold OCT− and OCT+ together for 5 seconds.
> - **Back to stock:** the installer's "Back to the official firmware", with M-VAVE's own `FM-1.fwsc`.

**What's in it:** nine presets (Feel, Gio, Mutant, Heaven, TickTock, Energy, Fly, Lover, Express), the lead on the
white keys, eleven effects on the black keys and all sixteen on the Effects page (FX), the Chords page (EDIT), the
next section (SEL), hold (REC), USB MIDI out and clock. The manual: https://guinanno1.github.io/fm1-gio/manual/

**Package:** `gio-0.1-alpha.fwsc` (its identity `FM-1_58` and the version), built and tested on GitHub by the shared FM-1 builder, with signed build provenance (see BUILDING.md to verify it).

GIO-1 is a fork of AMB-1 by Charles Vestal, built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp (Hügelton Instruments). MPL-2.0.
