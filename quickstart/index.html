Quickstart

MOR-1 on one page: every control, page, effect and song.

Start

  1. Switch on: Feel plays from its intro.
  2. PRESETS: another song, from the top.
  3. White keys: play the lead, always in key. Three notes and a pause: it answers, and keeps your line as its theme.
  4. Black keys: an effect while held. FX: all sixteen on the white keys.
  5. SEL: the next section. A tap on REC: stay in this one.
  6. EDIT: the section's chords, to change: KNOB 1 a chord, KNOB 2–4 its root, type and bars.
  7. ENV, KNOB 2: open the bass filter.

Controls

White keysThe lead, a step of the scale each; the chord's notes lit
Black keysEffects while held: Throw, Tape stop, Swell, Low-pass, High-pass, Sweep, Phaser, Freeze, Drop, Stutter, Roll
PLAYPlay / stop (the song from the top)
RECTap: hold the section. Held: the effects grid
EDITThe Chords page: the section's chords
SELThe next section, on the next bar
FXThe Effects page; again: the Space page
OCT− / OCT+The bass's octave
ALGORITHMThe bass pattern: Song, Pump, Pump 16, Arpeggio, Gallop, Octaves, Slow
PRESETSThe song (each keeps its settings)
SELECTPages. With HOME held: tempo 110–130
SAVESave. Held 1.5 s: the song's recipe again
