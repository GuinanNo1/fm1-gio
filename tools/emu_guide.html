Getting started

Press Switch on. MOR-1 plays the first song, Feel, from its intro. Turn PRESETS for another song, press a white key to move the song to that key, turn ALGORITHM for another bass pattern. EDIT moves on to the next section on the next bar; REC holds the section you are in.

This is the same code the FM-1 runs, compiled for the browser. What you save here stays in this browser; Factory reset forgets it.

Buttons and dials

KeysThe key: any key moves the song to its note. The keys light the chord playing.
PLAY / STOPPlays or stops. From stopped, the song starts at its first section.
RECHold: the section repeats until you press it again.
EDITThe next section, on the next bar.
OCT− / OCT+The bass an octave down or up.
ALGORITHMThe bass pattern: Song (the song's own), Pump, Pump 16, Arpeggio, Gallop, Octaves, Slow.
PRESETSThe song. Each keeps its settings as you left it.
SELECTThe previous or next page. With HOME held: the tempo, 110 to 130.
SAVESaves all the songs. Held for 1.5 s: the song back to its recipe.

Hold any button for a second and the screen says what it does.

Keyboard

White keys: Q–I and A–K (the first is F). Black keys: 1–0 and -. P PLAY, O REC, Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+, ↑ ↓ SELECT. Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel; Shift-click or right-click holds a button down.
